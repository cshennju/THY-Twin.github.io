THY-Twin: A Digital Navigation and Auditing Twin for Ultrasound-Guided Thyroid Ablation

Chengkang Shen1, You Zhou1, Hao Zhu1, Longqiang Wang2, XueJing Zhang2, Xun Cao1, Ming Jiang2, Yunjie Jing3, Yi Lin3, Zhan Ma1
1Nanjing University 2The Central Hospital of Wuhan, Tongji Medical College, Huazhong University of Science and Technology 3Zhongshan Hospital, Fudan University
Paper Code

Abstract

Background: Thyroid nodules are prevalent, and ultrasound-guided thermal ablation is an increasingly preferred minimally invasive treatment. However, effective ablation requires precise preoperative planning, accurate intraoperative needle navigation, and reliable postoperative assessment—tasks that remain challenging due to the lack of integrated three-dimensional (3D) visualization and quantitative evaluation tools.

Aim: We introduce THY-Twin, a digital twin framework designed to provide comprehensive navigation and auditing capabilities for ultrasound-guided thyroid ablation. THY-Twin integrates patient-specific 3D thyroid modeling, real-time intraoperative needle tracking, and postoperative ablation assessment within a unified platform.

Methods: THY-Twin comprises three interconnected components: (1) a Preoperative Static Twin that reconstructs patient-specific 3D thyroid models from brief 2D ultrasound sweeps for individualized surgical planning; (2) an Intraoperative Shadow Twin that continuously aligns real-time 2D ultrasound frames with the preoperative 3D model to maintain spatial correspondence during needle guidance; and (3) a Postoperative Treatment Twin that reconstructs and co-registers post-ablation ultrasound data with the preoperative model to quantify ablation coverage and treatment completeness.

Results: THY-Twin enables comprehensive 3D visualization and quantitative assessment throughout the ablation workflow, facilitating precise preoperative planning, accurate intraoperative navigation, and objective postoperative evaluation of treatment adequacy.

Conclusions: By integrating neural reconstruction, real-time registration, and volumetric analysis, THY-Twin provides a complete digital twin solution for ultrasound-guided thyroid ablation, enhancing both the precision and safety of the procedure.
